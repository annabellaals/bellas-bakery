Homemade Lemon Cake

Baked with love.

Soft and fluffy lemon cake is full of fresh citrus flavor.

Ingredients

Everything you need.

  • 200g unsalted butter, softened
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 3 large eggs
  • 200g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, and vanilla.

How to Make It

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar together.
  3. Add the eggs.
  4. Stir in lemon juice, and vanilla.
  5. Pour the mixture into the cake tin.
  6. Bake for 35–45 minutes.
