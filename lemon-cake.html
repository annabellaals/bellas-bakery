Homemade Lemon Cake

Baked with love.

Soft and fluffy lemon cake is full of fresh citrus flavor.

Servings
8 slices
Total time
60–75 minutes
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Everything you need.

How to Make It

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (160°C fan).
  2. Grease and line a 20 cm round cake tin with baking paper.
  3. Beat the softened butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  5. Mix in the lemon zest, lemon juice, and vanilla extract.
  6. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  7. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet mixture, adding the milk.
  8. Pour the batter into the prepared cake tin and smooth the top.
  9. Bake for 35–45 minutes, or until a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean.
  10. Let the cake cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer it to a wire rack.
